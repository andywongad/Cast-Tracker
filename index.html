Cast Tracker

Keep track of who’s who while you watch. Reality casts and long-running dramas throw more names at you than anyone can hold — Cast Tracker pulls in a show’s cast with photos so faces have names, and lets you write down what you actually need to remember.

  • Load the cast of any episode, with photos
  • Map the relationships — who’s interested in who, who’s allied with who
  • Spoiler-safe character bios and “previously on” recaps that stop where you have
  • Be told before a new episode airs

Your library is stored in your own browser, not on a server. No account needed.
